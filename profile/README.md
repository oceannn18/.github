# Team 4788, Can't Control
![4788](https://github.com/CurtinFRC/.github/blob/master/LongLogo.jpg)

## Who are we?
We are a community First Robotics Competition team based out of Curtin University from Western Australia (WA). Founded in 2013 we were the very first FIRST team in WA and have started a variety of FIRST programs here in WA. Along with ths we have also competed very succesfully in FRC for many years. In the 2025 season, we won the Impact Award at SCR, were on Einstein, and won Impact Finalist at Champs.
